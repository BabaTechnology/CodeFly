# Commercial Model

CodeFly keeps the model simple: Direct access is free because it does not consume CodeFly infrastructure. CodeFly Relay is paid because it keeps your hosts reachable through infrastructure that CodeFly operates and maintains.

Prices are shown inside the mobile app by the App Store or Google Play according to the user's platform, region, currency, taxes, and store rules. This document does not list fixed prices.

## Relay Subscriptions

CodeFly currently defines four Relay subscription tiers:

| Tier | Relay host seats |
| --- | ---: |
| Lite | 1 |
| Plus | 3 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Max | 20 |

A Relay host seat represents one computer that can stay reachable through CodeFly Relay. Direct access does not require a Relay subscription.

## Trial

CodeFly is intended to provide a 7-day free Pro trial by default. Exact trial availability, eligibility, renewal behavior, cancellation behavior, and billing terms are determined by the payment platform shown in the mobile app.

If a Relay trial or subscription ends, Direct access continues to work. Local provider sessions and host data remain on the user's computer.
